# decision-log.md

Version: 1.0

Project: Project Clarity

---

# Purpose

This document records the important decisions made during the creation of
Project Clarity.

It exists to preserve the reasoning behind the brand, website and user
experience.

Whenever a future change is proposed, this document should be reviewed
first.

If a proposed change contradicts one of these principles, it should be
challenged before implementation.

---

# Decision 001

## Build a Personal Brand Instead of an Astrology Brand

Decision

The website represents **Jinesh Savlani**, not a generic astrology
business.

Reason

People trust people.

Not logos.

Not company names.

The long-term asset is the personal brand.

---

# Decision 002

## Positioning

Decision

Use

**Engineer Turned Classical Vedic Astrologer**

as the primary positioning.

Reason

This immediately differentiates the brand.

Engineering communicates:

• Logic

• Structure

• Analysis

Astrology communicates:

• Wisdom

• Tradition

• Interpretation

The combination is unique.

---

# Decision 006

## No Fear-Based Marketing

Decision

Fear will never be used as a marketing tool.

Examples rejected:

• Bad Saturn

• Dangerous Rahu

• Your marriage is in danger

• Immediate remedies

Reason

Trust is more valuable than short-term conversions.

---

# Decision 007

## No Gemstone Business

Decision

Project Clarity will not sell gemstones.

Reason

The objective is guidance.

Not product sales.

---

# Decision 008

## No Miracle Claims

Decision

The website will never promise:

Guaranteed predictions.

Guaranteed results.

Miracle remedies.

Reason

Such claims are inconsistent with the values of the brand.

---

# Decision 009

## Education Before Prediction

Decision

Teach first.

Interpret second.

Reason

An informed client develops trust.

An uninformed client develops dependency.

Project Clarity chooses trust.

---

# Decision 010

## Website Style

Decision

Modern.

Minimal.

Calm.

Reason

The website should resemble a trusted professional rather than a
traditional astrology website.

---

# Decision 011

## Avoid Astrology Clichés

Decision

Do not use:

Galaxy backgrounds.

Crystal balls.

Golden zodiac wheels.

Mystical graphics.

Reason

These distract from the real value:

Thoughtful guidance.

---

# Decision 012

## Photography

Decision

Use authentic photographs of Jinesh.

Reason

The person is the brand.

Real photography builds trust.

---

# Decision 013

## Articles

Decision

Articles exist to educate.

Not to chase search traffic.

Reason

Long-term authority comes from useful content.

---

# Decision 014

## Website Size

Decision

Keep the website intentionally small.

Reason

Every page should have a purpose.

Quality over quantity.

---

# Decision 015

## Consultation Process

Decision

The consultation follows four stages:

1.

Consultation Request

↓

2.

Initial Conversation

↓

3.

Independent Analysis

↓

4.

Detailed Consultation

Reason

Careful preparation produces better guidance than instant readings.

---

# Decision 016

## Offline Analysis

Decision

Several hours are reserved exclusively for chart analysis before the
consultation.

Reason

Preparation is part of the service.

---

# Decision 017

## Communication Style

Decision

Write like a thoughtful teacher.

Never like a marketer.

Reason

The relationship is built on trust.

Not persuasion.

---

# Decision 018

## Website Goal

Decision

The website should help visitors trust Jinesh before asking them to book
a consultation.

Reason

Trust precedes every meaningful client relationship.

---

# Decision 019

## Design Philosophy

Decision

Content is the hero.

Design supports the content.

Reason

Beautiful design should never distract from meaningful ideas.

---

# Decision 020

## Long-Term Principle

Every future decision should answer one question:

"Does this increase trust?"

If not,

it probably does not belong in Project Clarity.

---

# Final Note

Project Clarity was intentionally built to be different.

Not louder.

Not more mystical.

Not more commercial.

Simply more thoughtful.

That principle should guide every future change.
