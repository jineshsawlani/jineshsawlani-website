# thank-you.md

Version: 1.0

Project: Project Clarity
Status: Page Specification

---

# Purpose

The Thank You page is displayed immediately after a visitor successfully
submits either:

• A Clarity Consultation Request

or

• A Contact Form

Its purpose is to reassure the visitor that their submission has been
received and clearly explain what happens next.

The visitor should leave feeling confident, informed and appreciated.

---

# Business Goal

Primary Goal

Provide reassurance.

Secondary Goals

• Set expectations.

• Reduce unnecessary follow-up emails.

• Continue building trust.

• Encourage further exploration of the website.

---

# Emotional Outcome

The visitor should feel:

✓ Thanked.

✓ Heard.

✓ Appreciated.

✓ Confident.

✓ Relaxed.

Never leave the visitor wondering:

"Did my form actually submit?"

---

# Page Structure

## Hero

Headline

Thank You.

Supporting Message

Your message has been received successfully.

Thank you for taking the time to get in touch.

---

## What Happens Next

Clearly explain the next steps.

For Consultation Requests:

1.

Your request has been received.

↓

2.

I will personally review your information.

↓

3.

If any additional information is required, I will contact you.

↓

4.

You will receive an email regarding the next steps and scheduling.

---

For General Contact Forms:

Thank you for your message.

I personally read every enquiry and will respond as soon as possible.

---

## Expected Response Time

State a realistic expectation.

Example

Please allow approximately 2–3 business days for a response.

This may vary depending on my consultation schedule.

Do not promise same-day replies.

---

## Helpful Links

Provide visitors with useful next steps.

Suggested buttons

Return Home

Read Articles

Learn More About Me

These links keep visitors engaged while they wait.

---

## Closing Message

End with a calm and personal message.

Example

I appreciate the trust you've placed in Project Clarity.

I look forward to connecting with you.

Warm regards,

Jinesh Savlani

---

# Design Philosophy

This page should feel calm.

Avoid:

Large illustrations.

Celebration graphics.

Confetti animations.

Countdown timers.

Promotional banners.

The visitor has already completed the desired action.

There is nothing left to "sell."
