# Purpose

The Articles page is the educational hub of Project Clarity.

Its purpose is not simply to publish content.

Its purpose is to build trust through teaching.

Every article should help visitors understand Classical Vedic Astrology
better while demonstrating the thoughtfulness, clarity and integrity of
the brand.

---

# Business Goal

Primary Goal

Build long-term trust through education.

Secondary Goals

• Improve SEO.

• Demonstrate expertise.

• Answer common questions.

• Encourage repeat visitors.

• Support consultation enquiries.

---

# Target Audience

People who:

• Want to understand astrology.

• Prefer thoughtful explanations.

• Are researching before booking a consultation.

• Enjoy learning.

• Appreciate traditional knowledge.

---

# Page Philosophy

The Articles page is a library.

Not a blog.

Articles should remain useful years after publication.

Avoid writing about trends or temporary news.

Prioritise timeless knowledge.

# Article Philosophy

Each article should:

Teach one concept well.

Use simple language.

Provide practical understanding.

Avoid unnecessary jargon.

Avoid sensational headlines.

Every article should answer one meaningful question.

---

# Suggested Article Structure

1. Introduction

2. Why This Topic Matters

3. Classical Understanding

4. Practical Interpretation

5. Common Misconceptions

6. Key Takeaways

7. Related Articles

8. CTA

---

# Writing Guidelines

Articles should be:

Educational.

Thoughtful.

Practical.

Evidence-based where appropriate.

Rooted in Classical Parashara principles.

The objective is understanding.

Not entertainment.

---

Understanding the Twelve Houses

What is Mahadasha?

How Accurate is Vedic Astrology?

Difference Between Astrology and Prediction

# Calls To Action

At the end of every article, encourage visitors to:

Learn More About Me

Request a Clarity Consultation

Explore More Articles

---

# Featured Images

Use clean, minimal imagery.

Avoid generic astrology graphics.

If no meaningful image exists, use simple branded illustrations or no
image at all.

Content should remain the focus.

---

# Internal Linking

Every article should link naturally to:

Related Articles

About

Clarity Consultation

Contact

Internal linking should support learning rather than SEO alone.

---

# Publishing Standards

Never publish simply to increase volume.

Every article should meet these standards:

✓ Accurate

✓ Clear

✓ Timeless

✓ Helpful

✓ Well-structured

✓ Edited

Quality always takes priority over quantity.
