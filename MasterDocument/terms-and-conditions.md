# About Project Clarity

Project Clarity provides educational content and one-to-one Classical
Vedic Astrology consultations.

The purpose of the consultation is to provide thoughtful guidance,
self-understanding and practical insights through the interpretation of
a birth chart.

A Clarity Consultation is a personalised service.

Each consultation involves:

• Review of submitted birth details.

• Independent horoscope analysis.

• Personal consultation.

• Discussion and interpretation.

Preparation requires significant time and individual attention.
