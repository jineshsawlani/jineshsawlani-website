# Purpose

The Clarity Consultation page explains the flagship service offered by
Project Clarity.

Its purpose is to help visitors understand:

• What the consultation is.

• Who it is for.

• How it is conducted.

• What they will receive.

• Why it is different.

The page should remove uncertainty and build confidence before asking the
visitor to submit a consultation request.

---

# Business Goal

Primary Goal

Generate qualified consultation requests.

Secondary Goals

Build confidence.

Explain the process.

Set realistic expectations.

Reduce unnecessary questions.

# Target Audience

People facing important life decisions.

People seeking clarity rather than certainty.

People who appreciate thoughtful conversations.

People willing to invest time in understanding themselves.

# Core Message

This is not a quick horoscope reading.

It is a carefully prepared Clarity Consultation based on Classical
Vedic Astrology, designed to help you understand yourself, recognise
patterns and make better-informed decisions.
