# Purpose

This document defines the writing style for Project Clarity.

Every piece of communication should feel as though it was written by the
same person, regardless of whether it appears on the website, in an
email, on LinkedIn, in an article or during a consultation.

---

# Writing Philosophy

Write to help.

Not to impress.

The objective is understanding.

Not admiration.

Readers should feel that they are having a thoughtful conversation, not
reading marketing copy.

---

# Overall Tone

The writing should always feel:

• Calm

• Thoughtful

• Honest

• Respectful

• Professional

• Warm

• Humble

• Clear

Never:

• Dramatic

• Aggressive

• Arrogant

• Sensational

• Fear-based

---

# The Voice

Imagine sitting across the table from someone seeking guidance.

Write exactly as you would speak.

Patiently.

Clearly.

Without rushing.

Without trying to sound intelligent.

---

# Reading Level

Write for an educated reader with no astrology background.

Explain concepts simply.

Avoid unnecessary Sanskrit unless it genuinely improves understanding.

If a Sanskrit term is introduced, explain it immediately.

---

# Sentence Style

Prefer:

Short sentences.

Short paragraphs.

Natural language.

One idea at a time.

Avoid:

Long academic paragraphs.

Complex sentence structures.

Marketing jargon.

Buzzwords.

---

Guaranteed

Miracle

Secret

Hidden

Magic

Shocking

Powerful

Life-changing

Lucky

Unlucky

Destiny

Destroy

Curse

Instant

---

# Explaining Astrology

Always explain before concluding.

Instead of:

"Saturn is causing this."

Prefer:

"Saturn represents responsibility, discipline and delayed rewards.
Because of its placement in your chart, these themes become more
prominent during this period."

Teach first.

Interpret second.

---

# Consultation Language

Clients should never feel judged.

Avoid:

"You made the wrong decision."

Prefer:

"This chart suggests that a different approach may produce better
results."

---

# Headlines

Headlines should be:

Simple.

Direct.

Meaningful.

Avoid clever headlines that require explanation.

Examples

Good

Thoughtful Guidance Through Classical Vedic Astrology

Understanding Your Horoscope

The Purpose Of A Clarity Consultation

Poor

Unlock Your Cosmic Destiny

Secrets Hidden In Your Stars

The Universe Wants To Tell You Something

---

# Calls To Action

Preferred

Request a Clarity Consultation

Learn More About Me

Read the Articles

Get In Touch

Avoid

Buy Now

Book Today

Limited Time

Act Fast

Don't Miss Out

---

# Grammar Guidelines

Use British English consistently.

Examples

Analyse

Organise

Recognise

Practise (verb)

Licence (noun)

Maintain consistent punctuation and capitalisation.

---

# Writing Checklist

Before publishing any content, confirm:

✓ Is it clear?

✓ Is it honest?

✓ Is it respectful?

✓ Is it educational?

✓ Is it free from hype?

✓ Does it sound like Jinesh?

✓ Does it align with brand-foundation.md?
