# Purpose

This Privacy Policy explains how Project Clarity collects, uses,
stores and protects personal information submitted through this website.

Protecting your privacy is an important part of building trust.

Project Clarity is committed to handling your information responsibly,
securely and transparently.

---

# Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the website, we may collect:

## Personal Information

• Full Name

• Email Address

• Phone Number

• Country

## Birth Details

• Date of Birth

• Time of Birth

• Place of Birth

These details are collected solely for the purpose of preparing your
astrological consultation.

## Messages

Any information you voluntarily provide through:

• Contact Forms

• Consultation Request Forms

• Email Communication

---

# How Your Information Is Used

Your information may be used to:

• Respond to enquiries.

• Prepare your consultation.

• Communicate regarding your booking.

• Improve the quality of our services.

• Maintain consultation records.

Your information will never be sold or rented to third parties.

---

# Consultation Data

Birth details and consultation notes are treated as confidential.

They are used only for preparing and conducting your consultation.

Client information is never shared without explicit permission unless
required by law.

# Cookies

This website may use cookies for:

• Website functionality

• Visitor analytics

• Performance monitoring

Cookies do not provide access to your personal information.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings if you wish.

---

# Analytics

Project Clarity may use website analytics services to understand:

• Visitor behaviour

• Popular pages

• Traffic sources

This information is collected in aggregate form and is not intended to
personally identify visitors.

# Data Security

Reasonable technical and administrative measures are taken to protect
your personal information against:

• Unauthorised access

• Loss

• Misuse

• Disclosure

No website or internet transmission can guarantee absolute security, but
Project Clarity follows industry-standard practices wherever practical.

# Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably
necessary to:

• Provide consultation services.

• Maintain business records.

• Comply with legal obligations.

Information that is no longer required will be securely deleted when
appropriate.
