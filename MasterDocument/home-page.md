# Purpose

The homepage is the most important page of Project Clarity.

Its purpose is not to explain astrology.

Its purpose is to establish trust.

A visitor should finish the homepage believing:

> "I would like to have a conversation with this person."

For overall brand philosophy, refer to **brand-foundation.md**.

---

# Business Goal

Primary Goal

• Generate Clarity Consultation enquiries.

Secondary Goals

• Build trust.
• Introduce the brand.
• Explain the philosophy.
• Encourage exploration of the website.

---

# Target Audience

The homepage is written for people who:

• Want thoughtful guidance.
• Prefer logic over sensationalism.
• Appreciate classical knowledge.
• Are looking for understanding rather than predictions.
• Value honesty and clarity.

---

# Visitor Journey

The homepage should naturally guide visitors through the following questions.

Who are you?

↓

Why are you different?

↓

Can I trust you?

↓

How can you help me?

↓

What should I do next?

# Homepage Structure

1. Hero

Purpose

Introduce the brand and establish trust immediately.

CTA

Request a Clarity Consultation

Secondary CTA

Learn More About Me

---

2. Engineer Turned Astrologer

Purpose

Explain the unique positioning.

Key Message

Engineering provides structure.

Astrology provides wisdom.

Together they create clarity.

---

3. Project Clarity Philosophy

Purpose

Introduce the values of the brand.

Key Messages

• No fear-based astrology.
• No miracle claims.
• No gemstone sales.
• Classical principles.
• Education first.
• Clarity before prediction.

---

4. Clarity Consultation

Purpose

Brief introduction to the consultation.

Explain:

• What it is.
• Who it is for.
• Why it is different.

Detailed information belongs on:

clarity-consultation.html

---

5. Who I Help

Purpose

Help visitors recognise themselves.

Typical situations include:

• Career decisions

• Relationships

• Life direction

• Business

• Personal growth

• Understanding planetary periods

---

6. Why This Approach

Purpose

Build trust without relying on testimonials.

Communicate:

• Personal preparation.

• Classical methodology.

• Logical explanations.

• Honest guidance.

• Individual attention.

---

7. Latest Articles

Purpose

Demonstrate expertise through teaching.

Show 3–4 recent articles.

Primary CTA

View All Articles

---

8. Final CTA

Purpose

Invite the visitor to take the next step.

Primary Button

Request a Clarity Consultation

Secondary Button

Contact Me
