# Purpose

The About page exists to answer one question:

**"Who is Jinesh Savlani, and why should I trust him?"**

This page is not a résumé.

It is not an autobiography.

It is a trust-building page.

By the time a visitor leaves this page, they should feel they know the
person behind the brand.

---

# Business Goal

Primary Goal

Increase trust.

Secondary Goal

Encourage visitors to explore the Clarity Consultation page.

---

# Target Audience

Visitors who are interested in the consultation but want to know the
person before making a decision.

These visitors are looking for:

• Credibility

• Authenticity

• Professionalism

• Integrity

---

# Visitor Questions

The About page should answer:

Who is Jinesh?

Why did he become an astrologer?

Why should I trust his interpretation?

What makes his approach different?

What values guide his work?

---

# Page Structure

1. Hero

Introduce Jinesh Savlani.

Simple portrait.

Short introduction.

CTA

Request a Clarity Consultation

---

2. My Journey

Briefly explain the transition from software engineering to Classical
Vedic Astrology.

The emphasis is not on leaving engineering.

The emphasis is on carrying analytical thinking into astrology.

---

3. Engineer Turned Astrologer

Explain why this positioning matters.

Engineering contributes:

• Structure

• Logic

• Analysis

Astrology contributes:

• Wisdom

• Interpretation

• Tradition

Together they create clarity.

---

4. My Philosophy

Summarise the principles from brand-foundation.md.

Key ideas:

• No fear-based astrology

• No miracle claims

• No gemstone sales

• Classical Parashara principles

• Education before prediction

• Trust before marketing

---

5. My Approach

Explain how consultations are conducted.

Mention:

Preparation

Careful analysis

Personal attention

Logical explanations

Individual guidance

Avoid excessive detail.

Direct readers to:

clarity-consultation.html

---

6. Lifelong Student

Communicate humility.

Mention that astrology is a lifelong study.

Emphasise continuous learning.

This increases credibility.

---

7. Final CTA

Invite visitors to continue.

Primary CTA

Request a Clarity Consultation

Secondary CTA

Read My Articles
