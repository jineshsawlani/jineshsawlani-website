# consultation-request.md

Version: 1.0

Project: Project Clarity
Status: Form Specification

---

# Purpose

The Consultation Request page is the entry point for every Clarity
Consultation.

Its purpose is to collect all information required before beginning the
analysis.

This page is intentionally simple.

It should feel like the beginning of a thoughtful conversation—not a
long application form.

---

# Business Goal

Primary Goal

Receive complete and accurate consultation requests.

Secondary Goals

• Reduce unnecessary back-and-forth emails.

• Collect all birth details.

• Understand the client's expectations.

• Prepare for the consultation efficiently.

---

# Visitor Expectations

By the time a visitor reaches this page, they should already understand:

• Who Jinesh Savlani is.

• What Project Clarity represents.

• How the consultation works.

This page should not explain the consultation again.

It should simply begin the process.

---

# Form Philosophy

The form should be:

Simple.

Professional.

Easy to complete.

Free from unnecessary questions.

Only ask for information that genuinely helps prepare the consultation.

---

# Form Structure

## Section 1

Personal Information

Fields

• Full Name *

• Email Address *

• Mobile Number

• Country

---

## Section 2

Birth Details

Fields

• Date of Birth *

• Time of Birth *

• Place of Birth *

• Gender (Optional)

Accuracy of birth details should be emphasised.

---

## Section 3

Your Questions

Large textarea.

Prompt:

"What would you like guidance on?"

Examples:

Career

Relationships

Business

Marriage

Life Direction

Personal Growth

Health (General Guidance)

Major Decisions

Visitors should be encouraged to be specific.

---

## Section 4

Additional Context

Optional.

Allow visitors to share anything they feel is important.

This often provides valuable context before analysis begins.

---

## Section 5

Consent

Checkbox

"I confirm that the information provided is accurate."

Checkbox

"I have read and agree to the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions."

Both checkboxes should be mandatory.

---

## Submit Button

Text

Submit Consultation Request

Avoid:

Book Now

Buy Now

Proceed To Payment

---

# Form Validation

Required Fields

✓ Name

✓ Email

✓ Date of Birth

✓ Time of Birth

✓ Place of Birth

✓ Consultation Question

✓ Consent

Email should be validated.

Date format should be consistent.

Time should preferably use a time picker.

# Data Handling

Store only information required for the consultation.

Never share client information.

Never use client information for marketing without explicit consent.

All personal information should be handled according to the Privacy
Policy.

Professional.

Comfortable.

No countdown timers.

No unnecessary distractions.
