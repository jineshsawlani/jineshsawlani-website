After Our Conversation

Many people wonder what they are meant to do once the sessions end. When something useful can be suggested, it stays simple and classical — for example:

  • Awareness of timing — when to push, when to wait, what the current period supports
  • Lifestyle adjustments — routine, speech, rest, or habits that match the chart’s themes
  • Simple classical practices — a personal prayer or mantra only if it fits you
  • Charity or seva — small, practical acts (including care for animals or those in need) on a given day

These recommendations are offered only when they are appropriate to your chart and circumstances. They are always optional, explained in plain language, and never presented as products or guarantees. They do not replace medical, legal or financial advice.
