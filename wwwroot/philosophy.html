Why I Practise

I do not practise astrology to predict the future. I practise it to help people understand themselves and their circumstances — so they can make wiser decisions.

When uncertainty takes over, people seek clarity more than certainty. Classical Vedic Astrology, applied responsibly, can provide that.

Core Principles

The Standards I Follow

Clarity over Prediction

Leave with understanding — not only a forecast.

Listen before Analysing

Your story comes first. The chart is studied after.

Explain before Concluding

Every important observation has reasoning you can follow.

Honesty over Certainty

If I am uncertain, I say so. Truth before the appearance of certainty.

Recommendations that Help

When guidance is useful — lifestyle, awareness, simple classical practices — I offer it. Never fear-based or commercial packages.

People before Payment

Fees are discussed openly. Sincere seekers should not be turned away by cost alone.

My Promise

You may not always hear what you wish to hear. You will always receive an honest, carefully reasoned opinion.

If astrology cannot answer something reliably, I will say so. Recommendations stay optional and practical — never a sales path.

What I Will Never Do

  • Create fear to gain trust
  • Guarantee outcomes astrology cannot guarantee
  • Push gemstones, expensive ritual chains, or dependency
  • Replace your judgement with mine

You should leave with greater clarity — not greater fear.
